### J2-009 · LOW · Criterion 3 (one consistent standard), 7 (consistency)
**Issue.** §1.4 says the same three questions apply to O1, O2 and O4 "and the §6 rating is built from them". O1 and O2 have function lists and published-claim maps (§2.1a F1–F6; §3.0(b) G1–G7). O4 has no function list. §6 therefore asserts "Published SME-priced direct competitor covering the core job: Yes — Axios" (l. 610) without mapping which O4 jobs Axios claims. §4.6 (l. 552) implies Axios does not claim some of them (Art. 7 request workflow with the two-month deadline, NCBA-level categories, output in the Italian ministerial format).

**Why it matters.** O4 is conditional and already rated the weakest (Weak), so the ranking is not affected. However, the orchestrator asked for one standard across all three candidates, and "covering the core job" for O4 is less rigorous than for O1 and O2.

**Exact correction required.** Add a short O4 function list (e.g., H1 Art. 5–6 pay information and recruitment; H2 Art. 7 request workflow with the two-month deadline; H3 Art. 9 indicators; H4 Art. 10 5% trigger and joint assessment; H5 job evaluation and equal-value categories, NCBA-based in Italy; H6 national-format output; H7 evidence/audit trail). Map Axios, Evenpay, Zucchetti and Figures to it from published claims, and restate the §6 O4 cell as "Yes, partly/largely — …".

## 4. Residual risks for Agent 3 (Strategy)
1. **The candidates are close on the evidence.** On one standard, O1 and O2 are both Weak–Moderate for under-service. Each has one published SME-priced competitor covering most of the core job: CVD Portal at €99/month for O1 and paiyroll at 15p/payslip for O2. Neither candidate has independent traction data or customer interviews. Agent 1's switch condition (O2 leads if SBOM-capable SMEs will not pay above free tooling plus €79–€99 offers) is still untested. The choice will rest on judgement about differentiation hypotheses (O1: all six functions at an SME price; O2: retention/immutability guarantee and an FWA-oriented pack), not on demonstrated demand.
2. **O2's remaining differentiators depend on future guidance.** An "FWA-oriented pack" has value only once FWA guidance on "adequate" records exists (planned in 2026–27; content UNKNOWN). paiyroll's free compliance check and payroll-audit service partly cover the arrears self-check. A government holiday-pay calculator is being considered.
3. **O1's remaining differentiator is thin.** CVD Portal lacks only automated SBOM↔CVE alerts at €99/month (the feature is in Enterprise), and CRA Evidence claims all six functions at an unpublished price. Either vendor could close the gap quickly.
4. **Low price anchors on both.** O1: €79–€99/month, plus free Dependency-Track and the SRP form. O2: 15p/payslip (£30 minimum); bureau payroll at about £1–£2 per client per month; employer full payroll at £1.50–£2.20 per employee per month.

6. **Sources to re-read before external use:** the Italian and Greek primary texts; snippet items [52], [72], [73], [86], [93], [94].
